

The Emergency Room Triage Priority Queue Using Max Heap in C++ is a project designed to manage hospital emergency patients based on the severity of their medical conditions. The system assigns a priority level to each patient and ensures that patients with higher severity are treated first. If two patients have the same severity, the patient who arrived earlier receives priority. Using a custom Max Heap data structure, the system supports patient insertion, severity updates, priority extraction, and viewing the next patient. It also simulates patient arrivals and treatments, calculates waiting times, and analyzes the time complexity of heap operations. This project demonstrates how heap data structures can improve the efficiency and fairness of emergency patient queue management.
